Chest pain accompanied by shortness of breath, sweating, or pain radiating to the arm or jaw may indicate a cardiac emergency and should be evaluated by a Cardiologist or emergency services immediately.

Persistent joint pain, swelling, or stiffness lasting more than six weeks is often evaluated by a Rheumatologist, who specializes in autoimmune and joint conditions.

Skin rashes, unusual moles, persistent itching, or acne are typically evaluated by a Dermatologist.

Recurring headaches, migraines, dizziness, or numbness in limbs may require evaluation by a Neurologist.

Abdominal pain, persistent nausea, or digestive issues lasting more than a few days are often referred to a Gastroenterologist.

Children under 18 with any of the above symptoms should generally be evaluated by a Pediatrician first, who can refer to a specialist if needed.
